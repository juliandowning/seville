Gary’s Insider Tips
VERMUT DE LA CASAEN RAMAGILDA PAIRING
Ask for vermut de la casa — best house-made vermouth, rarely on the menu. Try sherry en rama — unfiltered, straight from the barrel. Pair with gildas (olive, anchovy, guindilla pepper).

Señor Gary’s
Sherry & Vermouth Guide
Order “Vermut de la casa”
Often the best house-made vermouth in rustic, traditional bars. Never listed on any menu. Just ask.
Ask for “En Rama”
Try sherry in its purest, unfiltered form, served straight from the barrel. This is how it was always meant to be drunk.
The Perfect Pairing
Match drinks with classic tapas — Ibérico ham, anchovies, or gildas (olive, pepper, and anchovy skewers). Never wrong.
